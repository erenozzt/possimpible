Possimpible Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Who we are

Possimpible is a local-first daily mission app. Core progress, preferences, and proof media are stored on your device. We do not operate a user-account backend.

Apple Health / HealthKit

If you start a walking challenge proof, Possimpible may request permission to read today’s step count from Apple Health (HealthKit). We use this value only to verify that optional walking missions were completed.

  • Data read: today’s step count only
  • Data written: none
  • Not requested: heart rate, workouts, sleep, mindfulness minutes, and other Health categories
  • Purpose: verify optional walking-challenge proofs
  • Storage: on your device with the related proof
  • Sharing: not uploaded to our servers; not sold; not used for advertising

If you deny Health permission, the rest of the app remains usable.

Other data on this device

Mission history, streaks, XP, settings, and optional proof photos, videos, voice notes, or text stay on your device. Optional Game Center sharing of streaks, XP, and achievements uses Apple’s Game Center under your Apple ID. Location, camera, microphone, and motion are requested only when a specific proof needs them.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We do not share HealthKit data with advertisers or data brokers. Possimpible does not include a third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

Retention, access, and deletion

Because we do not host accounts, you can delete Health-related proofs and other local data in the app (Settings → Delete all saved proofs) or by deleting the app. Game Center stats follow Apple ID / Game Center controls. For access or deletion questions, contact the developer from the Possimpible product page on the App Store (App Support).

Children

Possimpible is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect HealthKit data from children.
